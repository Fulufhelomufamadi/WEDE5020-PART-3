Tshiawelo Primary School

Empowering Learners for a Brighter Future

Tshiawelo Primary School is a public primary school located in Tshitungulwane Village, Vuwani, Limpopo. We serve learners from Grade R to Grade 7 in a supportive and respectful learning environment.

Our goal is to help every learner develop the knowledge, skills and values needed for a successful future.
