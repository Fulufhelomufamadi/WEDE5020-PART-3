About Tshiawelo Primary School

Providing Quality Education for Every Child

Welcome to Tshiawelo Primary School

Tshiawelo Primary School is a public primary school located in Tshitungulwane Village, Vuwani, Limpopo. The school is committed to providing quality education in a safe, caring, and supportive environment where every learner is encouraged to reach their full potential.

Our dedicated teachers work closely with parents and the community to promote academic excellence, discipline, respect, and teamwork. We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to succeed through education and positive values.

Discover More About Our School

Tshiawelo Primary School fosters an environment that nurtures learners academically, socially, physically and emotionally so that they are equipped to tackle the challenges and become productive members of society globally.

To educate all learners to the highest levels of academic achievement, to enable them to reach and expand their potential, and to prepare them to become productive, responsible, ethical, creative and compassionate.
